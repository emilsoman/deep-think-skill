# Minto Pyramid

Source: https://untools.co/minto-pyramid/
Group: Communication
Use it for: making the answer clear: the main point first, then the reasons, then the details.

## Steps

1. Write the introduction with SCQ: **Situation** (what is known), **Complication** (what changed or what is wrong), **Question** (what we must answer).
2. Write the **answer** to the question in one or two sentences. This is the top of the pyramid.
3. Give 2 to 4 **key arguments** for the answer. They must be MECE: no overlap, and together they support all of the answer.
4. For each argument, give the **supporting data** or evidence.

## Result file

The SCQ. The pyramid as a nested list: answer → arguments → data. Mark the arguments with weak data.

## Your view in the chat

In later rounds, update your pyramid with the posts of the other agents. Post the current best answer and its arguments. Point out arguments that have no data.
