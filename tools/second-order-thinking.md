# Second-order Thinking

Source: https://untools.co/second-order-thinking/
Group: Decision Making
Use it for: finding the consequences of the consequences.

## Steps

1. List the main options or actions for the problem. If there are none, use the most obvious solution.
2. For each option, write the first-order result: what happens directly.
3. Ask "And then what?" Write the second-order results. Ask again for third-order results.
4. Look at three time spans: in 10 minutes, in 10 months, in 10 years (or: now, next quarter, in some years).
5. Look at other people and systems: who reacts to the change, and how?
6. Mark results that are bad now but good later, and results that are good now but bad later.

## Result file

For each option: a chain "action → 1st → 2nd → 3rd" and the time spans. Then the option with the best long-term result.

## Your view in the chat

Other agents look at the direct result. You ask "and then what?" about their best ideas. Warn about solutions that make the problem worse later.

## If it does not fit

This tool fits all problems that have actions. For a pure "why?" problem, apply it to the cause: what does the cause make happen next if nobody acts?
