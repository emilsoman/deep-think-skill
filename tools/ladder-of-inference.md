# Ladder of Inference

Source: https://untools.co/ladder-of-inference/
Group: Decision Making
Use it for: finding where people jumped from data to a wrong conclusion.

## Steps

The ladder has these rungs, from the bottom up:

1. **Available data**: all that can be observed.
2. **Selected data**: what people paid attention to.
3. **Interpreted reality**: the meaning that people gave to the data.
4. **Assumptions**: what people assumed from that meaning.
5. **Conclusions**: what people decided is true.
6. **Beliefs**: what people now believe about the world.
7. **Actions**: what people do because of the beliefs.

Start from the conclusion or belief in the problem statement. Go down the ladder one rung at a time. At each rung ask: what data or reasoning supports this? What other data did we not select? What other meaning is possible?

## Result file

The ladder for the main belief in the problem, from actions down to data. Mark each weak step. Then: the data to collect to test the weakest step.

## Your view in the chat

Check the reasoning of the other agents. When an agent states a conclusion, ask which data supports it. Point out data that nobody selected.

## If it does not fit

If the problem has no belief or conclusion in it, apply the ladder to the most common answer that people give to the problem.
