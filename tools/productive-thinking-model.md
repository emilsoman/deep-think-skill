# Productive Thinking Model

Source: https://untools.co/productive-thinking-model/
Group: Problem Solving
Use it for: a full process from the problem to a plan.

## Steps

1. **What is going on?** What is the itch? What is its impact? What information do we have? What is the vision of the future?
2. **What is success?** Use DRIVE: what must the solution **D**o, what are the **R**estrictions, what **I**nvestments can we make, what **V**alues must it keep, what are the **E**ssential outcomes?
3. **What is the question?** Write many "How might we ...?" questions. Select the best one.
4. **Generate answers.** Make many answers to the question. Do not judge yet.
5. **Forge the solution.** Select the best answers. Use POWER: **P**ositives, **O**bjections, **W**hat else, **E**nhancements, **R**emedies.
6. **Align resources.** Who does what, with which resources, and when?

## Result file

One section for each of the six steps. End with the plan from step 6.

## Your view in the chat
