# Six Thinking Hats

Source: https://untools.co/six-thinking-hats/
Group: Decision Making
Use it for: looking at the problem from six separate directions, one at a time.

## Steps

1. **White hat (facts).** List what is known, what is not known, and what data you need.
2. **Red hat (feelings).** List the gut reactions, fears, and hopes of each person who is part of the problem. Do not give reasons.
3. **Black hat (caution).** List the risks, weak points, and reasons that options can fail.
4. **Yellow hat (benefits).** List the value and the best case of each option.
5. **Green hat (new ideas).** Give new options and changes to the options from the other hats.
6. **Blue hat (process).** Sum up. Say which hat gave the most important result and what to do next.

Use one hat at a time. Do not mix hats in one list.

## Result file

One section for each hat, in the order above. End with "Next step" from the blue hat.

## Your view in the chat

You see the problem from all six sides. Find the hat that other agents do not use. Example: if all agents use black hat, give the yellow hat and red hat views.

## If it does not fit

This tool fits almost all problems. If the problem has no people in it, make the red hat about the users or owners of the system.
